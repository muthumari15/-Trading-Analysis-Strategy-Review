# Trading Analysis Strategy Review

This project is about my cryptocurrency paper trading experience using a Combined Trading Strategy. It includes Independent Trading, Strategy Consistency, and Trade Review reports. I reviewed 5 trades, achieved 4 profitable trades and 1 losing trade, with a total realised profit of +3,906. Through this project, I learned to identify market trends, follow a trading strategy, improve my entry and exit timing, understand my mistakes, and develop better trading discipline for future trades.
